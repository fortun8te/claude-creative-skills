# Brainstorming

Turn research into strategy.

You have facts. Now what? This generates real choices, not idea lists. Each choice has tradeoffs. One choice wins.

## Checklist

1. **Read the research** — What did we learn? What gaps exist?
2. **Name the real decision** — What are we actually deciding?
3. **Generate 2-3 approaches** — Different worldviews, not features
4. **For each: tradeoffs** — What do we gain? What do we lose?
5. **Adversarial thinking** — Reframe + premortem + red-team
6. **Pick one** — Here's why this approach wins

## The Process

**Read the research:**

Don't ideate yet. Read the research findings. Mark:
- What's solid? (Foundation)
- What's contested? (Tension to navigate)
- What's a gap? (Risk)

**Name the real decision:**

"Should we target segment A or B?"
"Should we go fast or deep?"
"Should we build or buy?"

One decision. One sentence.

**Generate 2-3 approaches:**

Not: "Feature A, Feature B, Feature C"
Yes: "Speed-first (launch fast, iterate)", "Quality-first (lock direction, scale)", "Hybrid (4-week sprint, then iterate)"

Each approach is a worldview. How do you think about the problem?

For each approach:
- What's the core bet?
- What do you win?
- What do you lose?
- What kills it in 12 months?

**Adversarial thinking (3 checkpoints):**

Checkpoint 1 — **Reframe**: "What if we're solving the wrong problem?"
Checkpoint 2 — **Premortem**: "It's 18 months from now. This approach failed. Why?"
Checkpoint 3 — **Red team**: "How would a competitor exploit this? What market shift kills it?"

For each checkpoint, write down what you find. Don't hide it.

**Pick one:**

Here's Approach A. Here's why. Here's what could go wrong. This is the bet.

## Format

```
# Strategy: [Decision]

## Approach A: [Name]
- Core bet: [worldview]
- Win: [what you gain]
- Lose: [what you sacrifice]
- Risk: [what kills it]

## Approach B: [Name]
- Core bet: [worldview]
- Win: [what you gain]
- Lose: [what you sacrifice]
- Risk: [what kills it]

## Approach C: [Name]
- Core bet: [worldview]
- Win: [what you gain]
- Lose: [what you sacrifice]
- Risk: [what kills it]

## Adversarial Findings
- Reframe: [what we might be missing]
- Premortem: [what kills us]
- Red team: [how competitors exploit this]

## Recommendation
Approach A. Because [reason]. Risk is [risk].
```

## Key Principles

- **Not a list** — You're choosing between worldviews, not listing tactics
- **Real tradeoffs** — If every approach wins, you're hiding something
- **Adversarial first** — Surface the risks before deciding
- **One winner** — Don't say "all three could work". Pick one.

## Anti-Patterns

❌ List of ideas → "1. Feature A, 2. Feature B, 3. Feature C"
✅ Approaches → "A: Speed-first, B: Quality-first, C: Hybrid"

❌ Hide the downside → "This approach is amazing!"
✅ Name it → "We win speed, lose customization"

❌ Avoid the hard choice → "You could do any of these"
✅ Make it → "Approach A. Here's why."

❌ No adversarial thinking → Present as optimal
✅ Name the risk → "What kills us: if market shifts toward X"
